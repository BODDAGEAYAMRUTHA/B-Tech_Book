Welcome to B-Tech Book (BTB)

This website provides an organized collection of resources for B-Tech students across various branches. Here’s what you can do:

  • Browse resources by branch: CSE, ECE, IT, EEE, etc.
  • Access study materials, lecture notes, and reference books.
  • Interact with BTB-Bot for quick guidance and support.
  • Log in and log out securely to keep your profile safe.

If you need further assistance, feel free to reach out to us via the BTB-Bot or the contact page.
